### 🏛️ Enterprise & Closed-Source Architectural Highlights

Because enterprise and nationwide public-sector solutions reside in private repositories, here is a summary of the architectural scope and engineering impact delivered:

* **Nationwide Land Service Gateway & Unified SSO System**
  * *Architecture:* Centralized identity and access management system authenticating citizen and government officer portals.
  * *Impact:* Implemented OAuth2/JWT security tokens with role-based access control (RBAC), preventing identity replay vulnerabilities across millions of citizen sessions.
* **Online Land Development Tax & Revenue Engine**
  * *Architecture:* High-concurrency financial calculation and collection engine handling transactional ledger integrity.
  * *Impact:* Optimized SQL indexing and Redis query caching to process large-volume payment requests concurrently, integrating seamlessly with government treasury and banking gateways.
* **Government Lease Management System**
  * *Architecture:* Multi-tiered workflow engine with automated state machines, audit logs, and document verification services.
  * *Impact:* Eliminated manual processing bottlenecks by digitizing inter-departmental approvals with end-to-end immutability and record tracking.
